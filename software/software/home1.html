About Kalidioscope

Kalidioscope is the creative unison of rich legacy, contemporary designs and sheer elegance. Established in early 1900s, Kalidioscope is one of the finest integrated woolen textile mills in Biella (Italy). During the course of time, Mr. Doady Vigna took over the mill and is now the principal architect of the company. Kalidioscope under his vast experience and expertise has grown to become of the first textile design house, producing and commissioning some of the finest fabrics available in the world, primarily from Italy.
